Hindsight Planning Lab

Two planners run the same learned world model, in real time, through three gates in a wind they cannot see. Hierarchical forward planning executes its subgoals as predicted. Hierarchical hindsight planning infers the hidden wind and motor gain from what each action actually did, and replans with that belief.

Inferred values come from the planner's belief about how its predictions are wrong: a gain multiplier on the predicted effect of an action, and an offset per step, shown here converted to an approximate constant force. Mass and damping changes have no slot of their own and are absorbed into these two.

Models: 5-member MLP ensembles trained in MuJoCo (seed 0 of the paper's experiments), exported as is. Physics, network outputs, belief updates and plan costs in this page were checked against the Python and MuJoCo code (errors of 1e-16 to 1e-7; demo/verify.mjs). Planner: CEM-MPC, horizon 15 steps (0.75 s), hazard penalty in rollouts; subgoals are the approach and exit points of each gate. Touching a red wall ends the episode. Dashed lines show what each planner predicted its current plan would do.
